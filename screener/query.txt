Screener.in query used to build the trading watchlist.
Run quarterly. Source of record for watchlist.json.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Market Capitalization < 15000 AND
Market Capitalization > 1000 AND
Sales growth 3Years > 15 AND
Profit growth 3Years > 15 AND
Return on capital employed > 15 AND
Promoter holding > 45 AND
(
    (Return on capital employed > 20 AND Debt to equity < 1.5)
 OR (Return on capital employed >= 15 AND Return on capital employed <= 20 AND Debt to equity < 1.0)
 OR (Return on capital employed < 15 AND Debt to equity < 0.5)
)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Intent
------
Profitable, efficiently run small and mid caps with founder skin in the game,
growing both top and bottom line, where leverage is tolerated only in
proportion to returns on capital. The debt tiering is the core idea: the more
efficient the business, the more debt it is allowed to carry.

Units: Market Capitalization in Rs crore. Growth and ROCE in percent.
Debt to equity as a ratio.


NOTE: the third debt tier is unreachable
----------------------------------------
The global filter requires "Return on capital employed > 15", so the branch

    (Return on capital employed < 15 AND Debt to equity < 0.5)

can never be satisfied. ROCE cannot be both above and below 15 at once. The
query therefore behaves as:

    (ROCE > 20 AND D/E < 1.5)  OR  (15 < ROCE <= 20 AND D/E < 1.0)

This has been left exactly as originally run so the current watchlist stays
reproducible. If the intent was to also admit low-ROCE, very low-debt
businesses, the global ROCE floor needs relaxing. Decide deliberately, then
record the change here with the date.


Why this is not fully automated
-------------------------------
Two of the criteria cannot be computed reliably from free data sources:

  Promoter holding   Not exposed by yfinance. Only `heldPercentInsiders` is
                     available, which is an insider-holding proxy and does not
                     match the SEBI-disclosed promoter figure.

  Debt to equity     yfinance returns inconsistent units across symbols. Real
                     examples: GARUDA 2.649, PRECWIRE 39.377, KSHINTL 39.74 -
                     a mix of ratios and percentages. Unusable for a threshold
                     test, and this field is the core of the query.

Market capitalization and 3-year sales/profit growth ARE computable, so
build_watchlist.py cross-checks those and flags drift between quarters.

The quarterly run therefore stays manual by design: export from Screener.in,
commit the CSV, and let the workflow do everything after that. See README.md.
